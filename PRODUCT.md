# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Stack

Flutter/Dart and SQLite, as chosen in PRD Final v1.0. No account or backend in v1.

## Users

An Indonesian warung owner aged 50+, with limited confidence using technology. Family assists with setup and backups on one phone.

## Product Purpose

Record customer debts, supplier debts and payments while serving customers, and explain remaining balances using a traceable history.

## Operating Context

Frequent short sessions in a shop. Works without internet. Family manually exports encrypted backups and keeps a recovery key outside the phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

PRD.md is the normative source. Two separate categories, integer Rupiah, installments, corrections, opening balances, archive, file restore. No inventory, sales checkout, account, cloud sync, PIN app, Excel or WhatsApp v1.

## Brand Commitments

Catatan Warung. Plain Indonesian, familiar labels. Android Material controls with large readable text and touch targets. The approved PRD specifies two clear home choices, no charts, no decorative onboarding. The user selected a blue-and-white finance-app reference for the visual treatment; preserve the existing routes and bookkeeping/backup behavior while applying its color, rounded surfaces, icons, and spacing.

## Evidence on Hand

PRD.md and PRD-lampiran-backup-cloud.md. No real financial data. Samsung model and OS still require verification; no physical-device success is assumed.

## Product Principles

- Record before the customer leaves.
- Never report success before persistence completes.
- Explain the source of a balance.
- Keep backup claims truthful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

18–20 base body text, 56dp primary controls, 48dp minimum touch areas, 200% text scaling, TalkBack, contrast at least 4.5:1. Product mode is practical and calm.
